01 / THE PROPOSITION

NOT A COURSE. NOT A DOWNLOAD.

A capability is not
a possession. It is a connection.

MORROW imagines a different way to access specialised ways of thinking. Faculties are provisioned through a spinal interface, exposed to agents through Brain MCP, and sustained by a market for unused neural edge capacity.

The interface can be removed. The skills can go dormant. The person keeps changing.

05 / THE LATTICE

A PORT THAT STAYS. A MODULE THAT LEAVES.

The interface
is removable.

In MORROW's imagined hardware, a small spinal dock is installed beneath the skin through a minor surgical procedure. The Lattice module clicks into that port, becomes the active interface, and can be removed without removing the port.

The Faculties remain provisioned while the module is connected. Unplug it and they go dormant. Your capabilities do not become your identity; they are arrangements you can enter and leave.

06 / CONTINUOUS BECOMING

YOU ARE NOT YOUR INSTALLED FACULTIES.

One person.
Many arrangements.

A researcher today. A builder tomorrow. A collaborator somewhere in between. The Faculties are not a final form. They are temporary relations between a person, a tool, an institution and a question.

One person can connect to many systems without becoming any one of them. Becoming is continuous. Access is conditional.
